How the models did across whole cohorts

Mean absolute error (MAE) in years with 95% bootstrap intervals. SALD is the only cohort that was not part of BrainAgeNeXt's training data, so it is the fair test of the under-4-years target.

What "ridge head" meansThe CNN is frozen. Features from inside it (512 or 1024 numbers per scan) feed a small linear model trained on IXI Guys+HH only. On IXI cases the head prediction is out-of-fold; on SALD the head never saw SALD.
Why IXI numbers carry a daggerBrainAgeNeXt was trained on openBHB, which includes IXI. Its IXI accuracy (3.38 y) matched its held-out SALD accuracy (3.39 y), so the overlap did not inflate it, but SALD is the clean number.
Limits of this demoCases are one per age decade plus each model's worst miss, per cohort. IOP scans are not shown. The tabular models only exist for IXI. One eNKI image was re-processed on the laptop with the same pipeline for display; every prediction is from the recorded runs.
